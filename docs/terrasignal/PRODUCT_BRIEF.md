# TerraSignal Investor — product brief

**Tagline:** See what others miss.

**Design rule:** One world. One AI. Almost no menus.

TerraSignal Investor is a mock-first globe for hunting distressed and off-market *looking* residential deals. It is a product fork of [God's Eye View](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view) (MIT). This phase does **not** call a live property provider.

## What Phase 1 is

A cinematic investor demo: globe → Atlanta / Decatur → mock signals pulse by type → the AI asks *Where are we hunting today?* Bottom chrome is only **WORLD · DRIVE · SAVED · AI**. Click or talk. No filter dashboard.

## What Phase 1 is not

- Not a listing product
- Not GPS / live drive-time
- Not OpenSky, Google News, TeleGeography, or other GEV live feeds
- Not investment advice

## Surfaces

| Surface | Job |
|---|---|
| World | Opportunity Vision on the globe |
| Focus card | Score, signal, value, equity, why this matters |
| Deal Vision | FLIP / RENT / BRRRR / WHOLESALE |
| Drive | Simulated route, strong signals only |
| Saved | `localStorage` bookmarks |
| AI | Existing GEV voice tools plus investor tools |
